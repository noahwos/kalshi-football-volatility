Football Volatility Analyzer ↗

Read the movement, not the winner.

  1. Choose a league, season and a checkpoint of 40 or 80 accepted observations. Only games held out from model training are shown.
  2. Sort by Forecast to compare predicted high-to-low home win-probability ranges over the next 40 observations. All range values use percentage points (pp).
  3. Select a game to see the probability path known at that checkpoint. The model estimates a range, not the direction of the next move.
  4. Turn on Reveal later observations to compare against the historical future. The orange line is the observed outcome, not a model-predicted path.
  5. Export the current filtered rankings to CSV. Observed future ranges are included only when the reveal toggle is on.

Model error is mean absolute range error across both checkpoints in the held-out season. Archive gaps, clock corrections and source-model revisions need review. CFB checkpoints count retained observations; the archive importer excludes administrative events. These results do not establish executable trading returns.
